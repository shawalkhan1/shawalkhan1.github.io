More about my research & current work

I build human-centered, trustworthy, and resource-efficient AI systems for social impact , particularly for communities with limited access to computation, connectivity, healthcare, and information.

My research lies at the intersection of Human-AI Interaction, Efficient & Edge AI, Responsible AI, and AI for Social Impact . I am particularly interested in making advanced AI accessible in resource-constrained settings while ensuring that efficiency does not compromise safety, fairness, cultural relevance, or usability.

I am a Full-Stack AI Engineer at Awaaz-e-Sehat , Pakistan's voice-first LLM-powered maternal healthcare assistant, developed at the National AI Hub , LUMS . My current work focuses on evaluating and developing multilingual AI systems for maternal healthcare, including fairness, safety, linguistic disparities, and efficient deployment in low-connectivity environments.

Research Interests

My research asks a central question: How can we design AI systems that remain capable, trustworthy, and useful when computation, connectivity, data, and expert access are limited?

Human-Centered & Efficient AI

I study small language models, model compression, quantization, efficient multimodal inference, and on-device AI , while examining how these constraints affect safety, linguistic fidelity, cultural understanding, trust, and usability.

LLM compression → Voice-first healthcare →

Trustworthy, Responsible & Agentic AI

I study the safety, accountability, and human consequences of increasingly autonomous AI systems , including LLM agents that reason, use tools, interact with other agents, and act on users' behalf. I am interested in fairness, robustness, sycophancy, human oversight, multi-agent behavior, and how agentic systems redistribute agency, responsibility, and power between users, developers, institutions, and AI.

Clinical fairness audit → Agentic systems →

AI for Healthcare & Social Impact

I develop multilingual, voice-first, culturally grounded AI systems for low-resource settings , with a focus on maternal healthcare and broader applications in healthcare, education, accessibility, and public services.

Awaaz-e-Sehat → BenchMaterna →

Human-Centered AI & Social Simulation

I study LLM-based agents and computational social simulations that model individual and collective behavior, with interests in behavioral fidelity, bias, diversity, and applications to public health, policy, and societal decision-making.

SYNQ → LLM Twin →

Overall, my goal is to develop AI that is not only powerful, but accessible, accountable, culturally grounded, and designed to enhance rather than diminish human agency.

Beyond the research

Mathematics and physics still shape how I think. I enjoy finding the same structure across different representations, and spending clear nights photographing the sky.

Mathematics, blackboards & the night sky
Classical Electromagnetism

One law, four languages

Maxwell's equations written four ways on a single board — integral form, differential (∇·, ∇×) form, the manifestly covariant tensor form ∂ α F αβ = μ₀J β , and the compact differential-forms statement dF = 0, d⋆F = μ₀J.

Seeing the same physics survive every change of notation is, to me, the whole point of theoretical physics — and exactly the kind of structural thinking I bring to AI.

Gear & method: long-exposure deep-sky imaging through a tracked telescope, with frame stacking and post-processing to draw faint structure out of the noise — the observational counterpart to the theory above.
