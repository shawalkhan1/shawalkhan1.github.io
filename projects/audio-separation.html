Deep Learning / Audio

Audio source separation

A U-Net and adversarial training approach to separating noisy and clean spectrograms.

Shawal Latif · PyTorch

Technical project

Problem My contribution Approach Evidence Limitations Next questions

The problem

Recovering useful signal from noisy audio is a representation-learning problem with a direct connection to voice interfaces.

My contribution

I built a U-Net CNN-GAN approach that separates noisy and clean spectrograms, using the GAN as a segmentation loss.

How it works

01 Noisy spectrogram
02 U-Net prediction
03 Adversarial loss
04 Separated signal

The design connects convolutional segmentation with adversarial training. The schematic summarizes the approach; it is not an audio-quality measurement.

Evidence & current status

The PyTorch implementation combines convolutional segmentation with adversarial training. Quantitative separation results and listening examples remain to be documented.

Limitations

A meaningful assessment would require a stated audio dataset, baselines, and objective and perceptual evaluation. Those results are not presented here.

Next questions

How can source separation support voice interaction without removing linguistic cues or introducing artifacts that affect downstream interpretation?
