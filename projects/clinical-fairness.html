Responsible AI / Multilingual Evaluation

Clinical fairness auditing of frontier LLMs

Testing whether clinical language-model behavior changes across linguistic forms and intersectional user profiles.

Shawal Latif, M. Mustafa, et al. · Collaborative research

Manuscript in preparation and submission / target FAccT 2027

Problem My contribution Approach Evidence Limitations Next questions

The problem

A model can perform well on an aggregate evaluation while behaving differently for particular language varieties or demographic profiles. In healthcare, differences in care and triage decisions need specific investigation.

My contribution

My work uses perturbation benchmarks across Roman Urdu, code-mixed language, and intersectional profiles. It introduces Reduced Care Rate, Reduced Care Error Rate, and Triage Threshold Shift to study clinical fairness.

How it works

Examine linguistic disparities.

Roman Urdu and code-mixed language are important parts of the evaluation. The audit asks how linguistic form relates to differences in model responses.

Examine intersectional behavior.

The study includes intersectional user profiles to look for behavior that a single demographic comparison may miss.

Measure differences in care.

Reduced Care Rate and Reduced Care Error Rate are proposed metrics in this work. Complete metric definitions and numerical findings belong in the manuscript.

Examine decision thresholds.

Triage Threshold Shift investigates variation in triage behavior. This is a research evaluation, not a clinical decision tool.

Audit-design overview. Full metric definitions and findings belong in the manuscript.

Evidence & current status

A manuscript describing the clinical fairness audit targets FAccT 2027. The available portfolio summary identifies the perturbation settings and proposed metrics, but does not publish the study results.

Limitations

The complete metric definitions, experimental protocol, and results are not included in this portfolio. A research audit also does not establish that a system is safe for clinical deployment.

Next questions

Does resource-efficient inference preserve equitable behavior across language varieties and user profiles? This is a direction for connecting responsible AI with edge deployment.
